Rygostancija by ZHRI Records
A sampler plugin for REAPER and other VST3 hosts on 64-bit Windows 10 or newer.

Installing
1. Unpack the archive: right-click it and choose Extract All. The installer won't work from inside the archive.
2. Double-click install.bat and allow the administrator prompt.
   If you see "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. It appears because the plugin has no digital signature yet.
3. Restart REAPER and look for the plugin under ZHRI Records.
   If it isn't there: Options > Preferences > Plug-ins > VST > Re-scan.

The plugin installs to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\Rygostancija.vst3.

Using it
Long samples sit on the low keys, short ones on the high keys.
Drag a knob with the mouse. Shift: fine control. Double-click: reset. Click a number: type a value.
The EFFECTS switch turns all effects off, leaving a plain sampler.
ONE SHOT always plays to the end. GATE plays while the key is held. LOOP starts again from the beginning while the key is held.

Music mode
Turn on MUSIC, then play a key or pick a sample from the list.
Close the Sample Lock: that sample is now on every key, in tune, starting from its root note.
Open the lock to go back to choosing.
